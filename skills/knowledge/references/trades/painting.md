# Trade: Skim coat, painting and finishing (walls and ceilings)

Graph tasks: `wall-finish`, `painting`, `ceiling-finish`. Typical values: the **paint and filler data sheets** win. For plaster repairs in wet areas, see `walls.md`.

**DIY level:** very good DIY. The result depends on preparation (≈ 80% of the work), light and patience.

## Surfaces and the right system
| Substrate | Prepare | Paint |
|---|---|---|
| New gypsum plaster / plasterboard | prime (to even the suction), fill joints, sand | dispersion matt |
| Cement or lime-cement plaster | dry, remove dust, alkali-resistant primer | mineral or dispersion (check alkali resistance) |
| Old painted wall | wash, sand glossy parts, fill, spot-prime | dispersion, or a stain-blocking primer for stains |
| Wallpaper | remove it (steam or wet), wash off the glue, fill | dispersion |
| Mould-affected | find and fix the moisture cause first, remove mould (not just paint over it) | anti-mould paint only **after** the cause is fixed |
| Wet rooms | primer + paint for humid rooms, ventilation | silicone resin or moisture-resistant dispersion |

## Skim coat levels (flatness of the finish)
- **Q2** standard finish (for textured finishes), **Q3** standard for matt paint (a thin skim over joints and surfaces), **Q4** full skim over the whole surface for **gloss, dark colours, raking light** (windows or spot lights along the wall).
- **Recommendation:** Q3 for most rooms, Q4 for walls with strong side light.

## Paint choice (EN 13300)
- **Wet scrub resistance class 1–2** for halls, kitchens, children's rooms. Class 2–3 is fine in other rooms. Low-VOC for bedrooms.
- **Gloss level:** matt for ceilings and most walls (hides flaws), silk-matt or satin for trim, doors and radiators.
- **Coverage:** typical ≈ 8–12 m² per litre per coat (≈ 10 m²/l). **Two coats.** Primer ≈ 8–12 m²/l.
- **Quantity:** area ÷ coverage × coats, + 10%. Example: 100 m² × 2 coats ÷ 10 m²/l = 20 l, + 10% = 22 l.

## Tools and materials
Filler (ready-mixed and a powder one for deep holes), skim compound, mesh tape, **sanding block/pole with 120–180 grit** (final 220), a **raking lamp** to see flaws, vacuum or extractor, primer, roller (short pile 12 mm for smooth walls; 18–22 mm for textured), a roller frame with an extension pole, an angled 50 mm brush, trays, masking tape (low-tack), drop cloths, a mixing paddle, a stepladder or scaffold tower, knee pads, safety glasses, FFP2 mask for sanding.

## Method
1. **Protect:** cover floors and furniture, remove sockets and switch plates (power off), mask windows and trim.
2. **Repair:** open cracks, fill dents and screw holes, apply tape over joints, skim where needed (thin layers, feathered edges). **Let it dry per the data sheet**.
3. **Sand** (120–180 grit), move the lamp along the surface to find flaws, and then vacuum the surface and walls.
4. **Prime** all new or repaired surfaces and strongly absorbent ones, **let it dry**.
5. **Paint the ceiling first**, then the walls, then trim, doors and radiators last.
   - **Cut in** (edges, corners) with the brush, then immediately roll the adjoining area while it's wet ("wet edge").
   - Roll in an "M or W" pattern, then finish with light strokes in one direction. Work in sections of ≈ 1 m² and keep a wet edge.
   - Do the **second coat** after the drying time (typically 4–6 h for water-based). Sand lightly between coats if there's grit.
6. **Remove the tape** while the last coat is slightly tacky (cut the edge with a knife).
7. **Clean tools** immediately. Dispose of paint residues per local rules.

## Conditions
- Temperature typically **+5 to +25 °C**, relative humidity under ≈ 80%. No direct sun on the wall or strong draught.
- Don't heat to dry quickly. Ventilate without a through-draught.
- **Plaster moisture:** new plaster must be dry per the maker (cement/lime plaster ≈ 1 day per mm, gypsum less). If in doubt, wait.

## Tips & tricks
- Mix several tins of one colour in a larger bucket for an even tone ("boxing").
- Use a **paint extension pole** for ceilings. Roll away from the light so shadows don't show the overlaps.
- Dark colours need a tinted primer or three coats.
- A sample patch of 1 m² in the actual light before painting the room.
- Paint **before** the floor and kitchen units, but do the **last touch-up** after the other trades.

## Common mistakes
- Painting damp or dusty surfaces.
- Skipping primer on patches: "flashing" (shiny or dull spots).
- Painting over mould without fixing the moisture cause.
- Lap marks from losing the wet edge.
- Masking tape left for days.

## Quality checks
- Raking-light check: no ridges or patches. Uniform sheen.
- Sharp, straight edges, no brush marks.

## Cross-trade
- Electrical and plumbing: plate fixings after painting, cut-outs flush.
- Heating: radiators are painted last or pre-finished.
- Floors: protect floors and avoid paint drips on new floors.
- Ventilation: running in humid rooms before painting.

## Element: preparing the surface
1. **Protect:** floors, furniture, windows. **Power off**, remove socket and switch plates, tape the edges (low-tack tape).
2. **Inspect with a raking lamp:** mark holes, cracks, loose parts, stains, mould.
3. **Remove** loose paint, wallpaper and flaking plaster (scrape back to a sound edge). **Wash** greasy or sooty walls. **Mould:** fix the damp cause first, then clean with a mould remover.
4. **Fill:** screw holes and dents with ready filler, **cracks opened to a V** and filled (flexible filler for moving cracks). **Deep holes in two layers.**
5. **Sand** (120–180 grit) when dry, **vacuum** the walls and the floor, **wipe** with a damp cloth.
6. **Stains** (water, nicotine, marker): a **stain-blocking primer** before the paint.

## Element: skim coat and joints
1. **When:** new plasterboard, patched walls, or walls with strong side light (Q3 or Q4, see above).
2. **Joints on plasterboard:** tape (paper or mesh), **filler in 2–3 thin layers**, each wider than the last (feathered edges), sand between layers.
3. **Skim:** mix to the sheet (smooth, no lumps), **two thin passes** with a wide trowel, the first to fill, the second to level. **Don't overwork** drying filler.
4. **Dry** per the data sheet (longer in cold, damp rooms). **Sand** lightly (150–180 grit) with a **raking lamp** to find ridges, then **vacuum**.
5. **Corners:** **corner beads** (metal or plastic) on outside corners, filled to their edge.

## Element: priming
1. **Choose** the primer for the surface (a deep-penetration primer on new plaster and filler, a bonding primer on glossy surfaces, a stain blocker on stains, an alkali-resistant primer on cement plaster).
2. **Apply** one even coat with roller and brush, **cut in** the edges. **No dry patches.**
3. **Dry** per the sheet (typically 2–6 h) before painting.

## Element: ceiling
1. **First**, before the walls. Ventilate without a draught, room temperature +5 to +25 °C.
2. **Cut in** the edges with a 50 mm angled brush (a strip about 5 cm wide), then **roll** with an extension pole.
3. **Roll away from the window**, in strips parallel to the light, keeping a **wet edge**. **Two coats**, the second after the drying time (typically 4–6 h).
4. **Check** with a raking lamp **while wet** and touch up before it dries.

## Element: walls
1. **A 1 m² colour sample** in the actual light before you commit.
2. **Cut in** corners, edges and around sockets with the brush, then **roll immediately** in **M or W strokes** and finish with light, same-direction strokes. **Work in sections of ≈ 1 m²** and keep a wet edge.
3. **Two coats.** For dark or strong colours a **tinted primer** and possibly a third coat.
4. **Tape off** trim and ceilings (low-tack) and **remove the tape while the last coat is slightly tacky**.
5. **Clean tools** at once. Keep **some paint** in a labelled tin for touch-ups.

## Element: trim, doors and radiators
1. **Remove** handles and hardware, or mask them. **Sand** old paint (180 grit), **clean**, **prime** bare wood or metal.
2. **Paint** with a **satin or semi-gloss paint** for trim and doors (hard-wearing acrylic or alkyd), **thin coats**, brush with the grain, **two coats**, sanding lightly (240 grit) between.
3. **Radiators:** only with **heat-resistant radiator paint**, **switched off and cold**, **sand and prime bare metal**.
4. **Dry** per the maker before closing doors or fitting hardware. **Keep doors ajar** while drying.
