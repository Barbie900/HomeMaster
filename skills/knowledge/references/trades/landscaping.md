# Trade: Garden design and landscaping (soil, lawn, beds, trees, hedges)

Graph tasks: `garden-design-plan`, `garden-groundwork`, `garden-planting`. For paving, terraces and decking see `outdoor-surfaces.md`, for sheds, fences and retaining walls `garden.md`, for irrigation `irrigation.md`. Typical values: local climate, soil and rules win.

**DIY level:** fully DIY apart from **earthworks with heavy machinery**, **tree felling of large trees**, **retaining walls over about 0.5–1 m** (engineer), and **electrics** (licensed). It is the cheapest area to start small and grow in stages.

## The order that avoids rework
1. **Survey and plan** (task `garden-design-plan`).
2. **Utilities, drainage and levels** (`site-drainage`).
3. **Heavy works and deliveries** (skip, excavator, concrete).
4. **Hard landscaping** (paving, terrace, walls, fences, structures, **sleeves** for irrigation, power, lighting and data **laid before surfacing**).
5. **Irrigation lines** (`irrigation.md`).
6. **Topsoil, beds and lawn preparation.**
7. **Planting** (trees and shrubs first, then perennials, **lawn last**).
8. **Mulch, watering plan and first-year care.**
**Soft landscaping is the last thing.** Machines and deliveries ruin planted areas.

## Survey (what to measure and note)
- **Plan on paper** (scale 1:100) with the house, boundaries, doors, windows, levels, falls and existing trees.
- **Sun:** note which areas get **≥ 6 hours** (full sun), **3–6 hours** (part shade) or **less** (shade) at summer and winter positions. Don't trust one afternoon.
- **Wind and frost pockets,** views, privacy, noise, neighbours.
- **Soil:** feel (sand, loam, clay), **pH** (test kit, typically a lawn and most plants like ≈ 6–7), **drainage test:** dig a 30 × 30 cm hole, fill with water twice, then time the third filling. **Sinking slower than ≈ 2–3 cm per hour = poor drainage**, so plan drainage or raised beds.
- **Compaction and rubble** from building works (probe with a rod).
- **Underground utilities, septic tank and soakaway** located. **Never plant or build over them.**
- **Rules:** protected trees and felling permits, hedge heights and boundary distances, invasive species, ponds, fences, structures (see `garden.md`).

## Groundwork (task: `garden-groundwork`)
- **Strip topsoil and store it separately** (heaps ≤ 1.5 m high, so it stays alive). Don't bury it under subsoil.
- **Level and shape:** falls ≥ 2% away from the buildings (≈ 5% over the first 2 m), gentle slopes for lawns (≤ ≈ 1:3 for mowing), terraces or retaining walls for steeper ones.
- **Decompact** the subsoil where machines drove (deep fork or a subsoiler), because roots and water can't pass compacted ground.
- **Drainage:** a drain or a soakaway for low or heavy ground (**check the permit and distances**), gutters and downpipes discharging to a proper outlet.
- **Sleeves** (empty pipes Ø50–110 mm, with pull cords, ends capped and marked) **under every future path, drive and terrace** for irrigation pipes, power, lights and data.
- **Topsoil:** spread **15–20 cm for lawns, 25–40 cm for beds** (minimum depths), leave ≈ 2–3 cm below paving edges for the turf or mulch, **rake level**, remove stones and rubble, and let it settle for a few weeks if you can.
- **Soil improvement:** compost (≈ 3–5 cm worked in for beds), sand for heavy clay (a lot is needed and it can make concrete, so prefer compost and raised beds), lime only if a test says the pH is too low.

## Lawn
- **Seed or turf?** Seed is cheap and takes 6–10 weeks to establish, turf (rolls) is instant and costs ≈ 5–10 times more per m².
- **Seed:** a mix for the use and light (sports and play, ornamental, shade), **≈ 25–35 g/m²**, sown in two crossing passes, **raked in lightly and rolled**, kept moist for 3–4 weeks. **Best sowing: late summer to early autumn, or spring.**
- **Turf:** lay on a firm, level, moist base, **stagger the joints** (like bricks), roll, water in. **Lay within 24 h** of delivery.
- **First mowing** when ≈ 8–10 cm high, cutting a third at most. **Mow height ≈ 4–6 cm.**
- **Don't** use the lawn heavily for the first season.

## Beds, shrubs and perennials
- **Right plant, right place** (sun, soil, wind, size at maturity). A cheap plant in the right place beats an expensive one in the wrong place.
- **Planting:** hole **2–3 times the width of the root ball and the same depth** (the crown level with the soil). **Loosen the sides**, backfill with the native soil (a bit of compost), **water in well**.
- **Spacing:** by the mature size on the label. **Don't pack plants** for instant effect.
- **Mulch 5–8 cm** (bark, wood chips, compost) over the soil after planting, kept off the stems. It cuts weeds and watering.
- **Weeds:** remove perennial weeds (roots) before planting. A membrane under gravel beds helps, but plants in membrane beds do worse.

## Trees and hedges
- **Distance to buildings and drains:** about **half to one times the mature height** (more for fast-growing, thirsty trees such as willow and poplar), and keep roots away from pipes and foundations on clay soils. **Boundary distances** are set by local rules.
- **Planting:** the same as for shrubs, with a **stake and tie (loose, ≈ 1–2 years)** for trees, and **mulch and watering** (≈ 20–40 litres per week in dry spells in the first two years).
- **Hedge:** plants per metre as the plant label says (for example ≈ 3–5 for typical hedging, 1–2 for large shrubs), planted in a **trench**, pruned in the first year to make them branch. **Check the height limit at the boundary.**
- **Existing trees:** a root protection zone (about the crown width), **no soil level changes, trenches or compaction** under it. **Protected trees need a permit** to fell or prune heavily.

## Seasons and care
- **Plant in autumn or early spring** (the soil is warm and moist). **Summer planting** needs a watering plan.
- **Year 1–2 watering is the difference** between survival and loss (`irrigation.md` helps).
- **Feeding** lightly in spring, **mulch** refreshed each year, **pruning** by species.

## Tools
Spade, shovel, rake (soil and landscape), wheelbarrow, level and string line, hose or marking paint, **mattock**, **tape measure and a 2 m straightedge**, roller, lawn spreader, pruning tools, saw, **a soil test kit and a rod for compaction**. **Rent:** mini-excavator or skid steer (with operator or training), turf cutter, plate compactor, tiller, stump grinder.

## Common mistakes
- Planting before the building works and the hard surfaces are done.
- **No sleeves** under paving, and digging up the terrace later.
- **Subsoil on top and topsoil buried.** Builders' rubble in the beds.
- Trees too close to the house or the drains.
- **No watering plan** for the first two years.
- Lawn on a waterlogged or shaded site.

## Quality checks
- **Falls** checked with water, topsoil depth with a spade, **sleeves** located on the plan.
- **Plant survival** and growth after the first summer, no standing water after rain.

## Element: paths and edges
1. **Width** ≥ 90–120 cm for two people, **falls ≈ 2%** sideways, **edges** (a haunched kerb, a metal or timber edge).
2. **Base:** **compacted gravel 10–15 cm** for foot traffic, **a bedding 3 cm**, **pavers or stepping stones**, **or a gravel path** with a **membrane and an edge**.
3. **Join** to the terrace and doors **at the same level or with a step**.
4. **Lighting** conduits laid before surfacing (`irrigation.md`, `smart-home.md`).

## Element: garden lighting (low voltage)
1. **12 or 24 V** LED systems with a **transformer outdoors (IP rating)**, **cable depth** per the manufacturer, **a switch, a timer or a twilight sensor**.
2. **Mains-voltage lights** and the **supply** by a **licensed electrician** (RCD, outdoor-rated cable, depth and protection per the rules).
3. **Avoid** light pollution: **downward, shielded**, **warm white (≈ 2700 K)**.

## Element: compost and soil care
1. **Compost bin** in a **shaded, drained, accessible corner**, **a mix of green and brown**, **aeration**, **maturation ≈ 6–12 months**.
2. **Soil improvement** each autumn: **compost and mulch**, **no deep digging on heavy clay when wet**.
3. **Test the pH** every few years and **adjust by test**.
