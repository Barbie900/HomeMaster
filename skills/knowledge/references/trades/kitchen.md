# Trade: Kitchen (design, services, installation)

Graph tasks: `kitchen-plan`, `kitchen-fit`. Values are typical: the **appliance and unit installation sheets** win.

**DIY level:** assembly and fitting of units are DIY. **Gas connection is never DIY.** Panel and electrical connections need a licensed electrician where required. Stone and quartz worktops are templated and cut by the supplier.

## Ergonomics and layout (typical)
- **Work triangle** (sink, hob, fridge): each side ≈ 1.2–2.7 m, total ≈ 4–7 m, with no traffic through it.
- **Worktop height** ≈ 85–95 cm. Rule: elbow height minus 10–15 cm for the main user.
- **Clearance between facing runs:** ≥ 100–120 cm (≥ 120 cm for two cooks), absolute minimum ≈ 90 cm.
- **Wall units:** bottom ≈ 50–65 cm above the worktop, depth 30–35 cm.
- **Landing space:** ≥ 40–60 cm each side of the hob, ≥ 40 cm next to the fridge and oven.
- **Worktop between sink and hob** ≥ 60–90 cm (the main prep area).
- Doors and appliance doors must open fully (check the swing).

## Services (what to rough in, and where)
| Item | Water | Drain | Power | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sink | cold + hot, angle valves | DN40–50 with a trap | none | disposer or filter: a separate socket |
| Dishwasher | cold, angle valve | into the sink trap (non-return) or a separate DN40–50 | 16 A circuit | next to the sink |
| Washing machine | cold, angle valve | standpipe DN50 | 16 A circuit | check the noise and vibration, a drip tray |
| Hob (induction) | none | none | dedicated, commonly 3-phase 16 A (5 × 2.5 mm²) | confirm in the maker's sheet |
| Built-in oven | none | none | dedicated or 16 A socket | heat-resistant |
| Extractor hood | none | none | socket in the hood zone | duct DN125–150 |
| Fridge/freezer | none | none | socket (accessible, not behind) | ventilation gap |
| Microwave, small appliances | none | none | 2–4 double sockets at 105–115 cm | on a separate circuit from the hob |
- Position stubs from the **final kitchen drawing** (`kitchen-plan`), with the appliance **models**.
- Power: dedicated circuits for oven/hob, dishwasher, washing machine, fridge, and one or two circuits for the worktop sockets. RCD protection as the local rules say.
- Mark the services on the finished walls with photos before the units go in.

## Extraction
- **Duct to the outside is best:** rigid smooth pipe DN125–150, short (≤ 4–6 m), few bends, **sloping down to the outside**, with a wall box with a flap.
- **Recirculation** (carbon filter) is a compromise: it doesn't remove humidity.
- **Distance hob to hood:** per the hood's and hob's sheets (typically ≈ 65–75 cm over induction, more over gas).
- **Make-up air:** a window contact or a supply route. **Open-flue appliances** (gas boiler, wood stove) in the same room or air volume require a **safety interlock or a professional's check**. Back-draught can pull flue gases (CO) into the room.
- Never connect the extractor to a bathroom shaft, a chimney or an attic space.

## Fitting method
1. **Check the delivery** (count, damage) and store it flat and dry. Don't open the worktops yet.
2. **Mark the level line** with a laser: the floor high point + 90 cm (worktop) and the wall unit height. The **highest floor point** sets the plinth height.
3. **Wall unit rail** (if the system uses one): fix into solid structure with the right anchors (solid brick: plugs, hollow: through-bolts or spreader anchors, drywall: onto the backing). Wall units hold heavy loads (≈ 30–60 kg each when loaded).
4. **Assemble the base units**, set the feet to level in both directions, fix to the wall and to each other with the connectors. Leave service holes for the pipes.
5. **Appliances:** fit the dishwasher, oven and hob housings with the maker's ventilation gaps.
6. **Wall units** on the rails with the suspension adjusted, then the extractor.
7. **Worktop:** dry-fit, cut the sink and hob openings from the templates (jigsaw with a fine blade and masking tape), **seal every cut edge** (chipboard core) with the supplied sealant, fit the joint with the connecting bolts and sealant. **A stone worktop is templated after the base units are in.**
8. **Sink and taps**, connection and **leak test** (run the water 10 minutes, check the trap with paper).
9. **Electrical connection** (licensed where required) and **commissioning** of appliances. Test the RCD.
10. **Plinths, end panels, handles, silicone** at the wall–worktop and the splashback joints.

## Tools
Laser level, spirit level, drill/driver, jigsaw, circular saw with a guide (worktop), hole saws (Ø35 mm for hinges, 68 mm for boxes), clamps, plumber's wrench, pipe cutter, silicone gun, stud finder/cable detector, the right anchors, a trolley and two helpers.

## Common mistakes
- Stubs at the wrong height or wall position because the plan was not final.
- Not finding the true floor high point: uneven wall units and gaps.
- Unsealed worktop cut-outs: swelling.
- Wall units on the wrong anchors, hung on drywall without backing.
- Extractor connected into a short duct with a 90° turn right at the hood.
- A floating floor laid under the units: it can't move and buckles. Tile or glue the floor under the units, or lay the floating floor around them.

## Quality checks
- Units level and plumb (±1 mm/m), uniform gaps (2–3 mm), doors aligned.
- No leaks after the 10-minute test and after a full dishwasher cycle.
- Extractor airflow check and a safe, tested electrical installation.

## Cross-trade
- Plumbing and electrical rough-in positions, wall flatness (units need level walls), floor level (units, dishwasher, plinth), tiling or painting of the splashback, ventilation duct, and the gas rules.

## Element: sink, tap and waste
**Rough-in part**
1. **Position** from the final drawing: the sink centre, the **drain stub DN40–50** (centre ≈ 50–60 cm above the floor, ≈ 10–15 cm from the wall) and the **hot and cold angle valves** (≈ 50–60 cm above the floor, 15 cm apart, **hot on the left**).
2. **Waste:** **fall ≥ 1–2%** to the stack, **no horizontal 90° bends**, a **trap (siphon)** with a water seal (≥ 50 mm) and an **access** (inspection).
3. **Test:** water test of the drain, **pressure test** the supply (`plumbing.md`). **Cap** the ends. **Photograph** with a tape measure.
**Fit part**
4. **Sink:** the cut-out template, **sealed edges** on a chipboard worktop, the **sink clamps** and **sealant** around the rim. **Level**.
5. **Tap:** the **flexible hoses** (rated, one end ½"), **a gasket under the tap**, a **mixer set to the hot and cold** correct. **Flush the lines** (remove the aerator) before use.
6. **Waste kit:** **strainer, overflow, trap** with the right gaskets. **Hand-tight and a quarter turn.**
7. **Test:** **fill the sink and drain it** while you check every joint with **dry paper** for 10 minutes. **Isolation valves** accessible.

## Element: dishwasher and washing machine
**Rough-in part**
1. **Next to the sink**, in a **base unit or a niche** with the **width of the machine + 1–2 cm**. **Water:** a **cold angle valve** (a dishwasher normally takes cold), **drain:** a **spigot on the sink trap** (with a loop or a high connection that prevents back-flow) or a **standpipe DN50 with a trap**, **a dedicated 16 A socket** reachable (**not behind the machine**).
2. **Aquastop (leak protection) hoses** and a **drip tray** for the washing machine.
**Fit part**
3. **Level** with the feet, **don't kink the hoses**, **fit the door panel** and the plinth. **Fix** the dishwasher to the worktop underside so it doesn't tip.
4. **Test:** a **full cycle** and **dry paper** under the connections.

## Element: induction hob and oven
**Rough-in part**
1. **Dedicated circuits** (the electrician): an **induction hob** typically on its own **three-phase circuit (5 × 2.5 mm²) with a C16 breaker**, **an oven** on its own **circuit** or a socket suited to its rating. **Check** the **panel capacity** (`panel-and-main-services`).
2. **Positions** from the drawing: the **hob cut-out** and the **space for the cable** (a connector box in the unit **reachable**), **ventilation gaps** at the back as the oven maker says.
**Fit part**
3. **Cut-out** with a jigsaw and a template, **seal the edges** (chipboard), **fit the hob with its seal**, **min. distance** to the wall and to the hood **as the maker says** (typically ≈ 65–75 cm to the hood over induction).
4. **Connection by the electrician.** **No gas by DIY.** **The oven** slides into the housing and is fixed to the unit.
5. **Test** all zones and the oven's first heating (a first **burn-in** at an open window, as the maker says).

## Element: extractor hood
**Rough-in part**
1. **Duct DN125–150** (rigid, smooth) to the outside, **short (≤ 4–6 m)**, **few bends**, **sloping down to the outside**, a **wall box with a flap**, **make-up air** planned, **no connection to a bathroom shaft**.
2. **Power** (a socket in the hood zone) and the **mounting backing** (a hood weighs ≈ 15–40 kg).
**Fit part**
3. **Height** as the hood and hob sheets say (**≈ 65–75 cm** over induction, more over gas), **fix to the backing**, **connect the duct** with **airtight aluminium tape** and clips.
4. **Open-flue appliances in the same air volume** (gas boiler, wood stove): **a safety interlock or a specialist check**.
5. **Test** the airflow, the lights and the **return flap** (it closes when off).

## Element: base units
1. **Find the highest point of the floor** with a laser or a long level. **Mark the worktop height** (≈ 90 cm, or the user's height minus 10–15 cm) and the **plinth line**.
2. **Assemble** the units on a clean surface (a flat pack: **check the square** and the diagonals), **fit the legs**.
3. **Place** the corner unit first, **level both ways**, **screw to the wall** with the **hangers** into the structure, **clamp the units together** (screws or clamps through the sides).
4. **Cut the service holes** (water, drain, power) **before** setting the unit in place, **sealing the edges**.
5. **Fit** the doors, drawers and their **adjustment** (2–3 mm even gaps).

## Element: wall units
1. **Check the wall:** solid brick, hollow, or **drywall with backing**. **Choose the fixings** for **each unit's loaded weight (≈ 30–60 kg)**.
2. **Mark the hanging line** with a laser: the bottom ≈ **50–65 cm above the worktop**, and **fix the rail** (if used) at the exact height and level.
3. **Hang the units** from the **corner**, **adjust the hangers** (height, depth, tilt) and **screw the units together**.
4. **Load** only after the **adjustment and the check** (shake test).
5. **Extractor, lights and the filler panels** last.

## Element: worktop
1. **Measure and cut** (a laminate or timber worktop) on the **underside with a fine blade** and tape on the cut line. **Seal every cut edge** (the **chipboard core swells**).
2. **Joints:** **a mitre or a butt joint** with **connecting bolts and sealant**, **a 1–2 mm tight joint**, the **expansion gap at the wall** (≈ 3–5 mm) covered by a **trim**.
3. **Stone or quartz:** **template after the base units are fixed**, the supplier cuts the **sink and hob openings** (**don't cut them yourself**), **lift with 2–4 people** and **suction lifters**.
4. **Fix** the worktop to the units with **brackets** (a **slot for movement** for solid wood).
5. **Splashback joint:** **silicone** (colour matched) or a **trim**.

## Element: plinths, lights and handles
1. **Plinths** (toe kick): **clips** to the legs, **cut to length**, a **gap of ≈ 2–3 mm** to the floor, **vent grilles** by the oven and the fridge (if the maker asks).
2. **Under-cabinet lights:** **low-voltage LED strips** (12 or 24 V) with a **driver accessible**, **a sensor or a switch**, **the cable hidden in a channel**.
3. **Handles:** a **template (jig)** for even drilling, **drill from the front** onto a **backing board** to avoid splintering.
4. **Final adjustment** of all doors and drawers after the first week.
