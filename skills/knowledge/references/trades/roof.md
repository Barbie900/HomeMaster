# Trade: Roof and attic

Graph tasks: `roof-assessment`, `roof-repair`, `attic-conversion`. Typical values: the **covering and underlay makers' instructions**, your country's building and fall-protection rules, and an **engineer's opinion for structure** win.

**DIY level, honestly:** inspection from the ground and from inside the attic is DIY. **Falls from roofs and ladders kill more DIYers than any other renovation job.** DIY only with proper fall protection (scaffold with edge protection, or a roof ladder, harness and a certified anchor, plus a second person). **Hire a roofer** for: full re-roofing, structural timber repair, chimney and valley work, dormers, anything on a steep or high roof, and **any asbestos-cement roof** (never walk on, drill, cut or pressure-wash it). Roof windows in an existing roof change the structure: engineer.

## Anatomy (so you can talk to a roofer)
Rafters or trusses (structure) → **underlay** (sarking membrane, waterproof and vapour-open) → **counter-battens** (drain and ventilation channel) → **battens** → **covering** (clay or concrete tiles, fibre-cement, metal, slate, bitumen sheet) + **flashings** (chimney, valleys, walls, roof windows) + ridge and hip caps + **gutters**.
- **Cold roof:** insulation between or under the rafters with a **ventilated air gap (typically ≥ 4 cm)** under the underlay, open at the eaves and the ridge.
- **Warm roof:** the insulation fills the whole rafter depth, the underlay is **very vapour-open**, with an airtight, vapour-control layer inside. The detailing must be right.
- **Minimum pitch** depends on the covering and the underlay (tiles typically ≥ 22–25° without extra measures). Check the maker.

## Inspection checklist (task: `roof-assessment`)
1. **From outside (binoculars, drone or neighbour's view):** missing, cracked or slipped tiles, moss and lichen, sagging ridge or roof plane, flashing gaps, chimney mortar, gutters and downpipes, rot at the eaves.
2. **From inside the attic (daylight test, torch, and a dry day first):** daylight where it shouldn't be, water stains or drip marks, dark mould, **timber moisture (≥ 20% on a moisture meter = risk)**, soft rot (screwdriver test), insect holes, rusty nails (condensation), sagging rafters or purlins, cut or notched timbers, insulation condition, ventilation openings blocked, the chimney and the electrical cables.
3. **After rain:** where is the water getting in? A small leak in the ceiling is often far from the roof leak.
4. **Document** with photos and a dated list. Rank: urgent (leaks), important (condition), cosmetic.
- **Condensation vs. leak:** damp on the underside of the underlay or the roof deck in winter, no stain after rain = condensation. Fix ventilation and the vapour-control layer, not the roof covering.

## Safe working (non-negotiable)
- **Scaffold with edge protection** (rented, erected by a trained person) is the best. Otherwise **roof ladder** hooked over the ridge, **harness and anchor point** (certified), a **helper on the ground**, **non-slip shoes**, no work alone.
- **Never** on wet, frosty, windy or snowy roofs. **Never** on fragile roofs (fibre-cement, polycarbonate, rotten boards) without crawl boards.
- **Overhead power lines**: keep a safe distance. **Tools and tiles** secured against falling, and the ground area below closed off.

## DIY-appropriate repairs (from a scaffold or a safe access)
- **Replace single broken tiles:** lift the tiles above, remove the clip or nail, slide a new tile in, clip back. Keep a few spares from the same batch.
- **Re-bed ridge tiles** or fit a **dry-ridge system**. Re-point only if the mortar is the problem.
- **Small flashing repairs** (lead or metal strips, sealing tape rated for roofs, not general silicone).
- **Underlay patch** from the inside (an airtight patch with tape rated for it).
- **Gutters:** clean, re-fit brackets, repair joints, check falls (≈ 3–5 mm per m) and downpipe discharge.
- **Moss:** soft brush or biocide rated for roofs. **No pressure washing** (forces water under the tiles).

## Attic conversion (task: `attic-conversion`)
**Permit and rules first**, because change of use usually needs a permit and the loads change.
- **Headroom:** the rules often require a minimum clear height (typically ≥ 2.2–2.5 m) over a **minimum share of the floor area**. Measure and map the usable area (with knee walls).
- **Structure:** engineer for the floor joists (loads for living use are higher than for storage), rafters (new openings, roof windows), the stair opening and the collar ties.
- **Insulation and vapour control:** typically the full rafter depth plus an extra layer below or above (**≈ 20–30 cm total**), a **continuous airtight vapour-control layer on the warm side, taped at every joint and edge**, with a service cavity (≈ 3–5 cm) so you don't puncture it with cables and spotlights. Keep the ventilation gap (cold roof) or use a vapour-open warm-roof detail.
- **Summer overheating:** the attic is the hottest room. Use **external shading** on roof windows, dense insulation (wood fibre, dense mineral wool), light colours, cross-ventilation, and a ventilation plan. **Internal blinds don't work well.**
- **Roof windows:** size for daylight (≈ 10–15% of floor area), the escape route, flashing kits, and insulation collar kits. Install per the maker. Cutting rafters needs an engineer.
- **Stairs and escape:** a fixed stair (see `stairs-indoor.md`) and an escape window or route per the rules.
- **Services:** heating, ventilation, electrical, plumbing risers, and the sound from rain.
- **Interior:** drywall (see `drywall.md`), skim and paint (`painting.md`), floor (`flooring.md`).

## Tools and PPE
Safety harness and anchor, helmet, non-slip footwear, gloves, tile lifter/slate ripper (for replacements), tile cutter or grinder, tape and membrane patches, sealing tape rated for roofs, moisture meter, torch, binoculars, ladder with stabiliser, scaffold (rent and have it erected), FFP2 mask for old insulation, dust sheets.

## Common mistakes
- Working on the roof alone or without edge protection.
- Fixing the symptom (the ceiling stain) and not the source.
- Insulating a leaking or condensing roof, hiding the damage.
- No vapour-control layer on the warm side, or one that's full of holes from spotlights.
- Blocking the ventilation gap with insulation.
- Treating an asbestos-cement roof as ordinary.
- Pressure-washing tiles.

## Quality checks
- Rain-tight after the first heavy rain (check the attic).
- Moisture readings on timber stay low through a winter.
- Airtightness: a smoke pencil at the joints, or a blower-door test for a certified result.

## Element: replacing broken tiles
**From a scaffold or with certified fall protection only.**
1. **Find** the broken tile from inside the attic (daylight) and from outside. **Count** the rows to the tile.
2. **Lift** the tiles above with a **slate ripper or a lifting wedge**, **remove the nails or clips**.
3. **Slide out** the broken tile, **slide in a new one of the same type**, **clip it** or **nail it** as the others.
4. **Check** that the **underlay** below is intact (patch it from the inside if torn). **Bed** the tile in the right overlap.
5. **Keep** a few **spares** of each type. **Don't walk on tiles** (use a roof ladder or crawling boards).

## Element: flashings, chimney and valleys
1. **Look for** gaps, lifted lead or zinc, **cracked mortar fillets**, **missing sealant**.
2. **Small repairs:** a **flashing tape rated for roofs**, **a mortar-free (dry) detail**, **lead or zinc strips** worked in and **chased into the masonry joint**, sealed with **a roofing sealant** in the chase.
3. **Valleys:** **clean debris**, **patch** with a **valley repair strip** or a roofer's work if the lining is damaged.
4. **Chimney head:** **cap**, **mortar crown**, **flashing**. **A roofer** for big repairs.
5. **Water test** with a hose from the bottom up, **a person inside the attic** watching.

## Element: gutters and downpipes
1. **Clean** the gutters (gloves, a scoop, a hose), **leaf guards** if trees are near.
2. **Fall ≈ 3–5 mm per m** to the outlet. **Re-fix brackets** (spacing ≈ 50–60 cm), **new joint seals**.
3. **Downpipes** secured with clips every ≈ 1.5–2 m, **connected to a permitted outlet** (`site-drainage`), **an inspection or a silt trap**.
4. **Water test** with a hose and check **joints, corners and the discharge**.

## Element: roof windows
1. **Structural:** the **opening cuts rafters** (**an engineer or the window maker's guidance, trimmers**).
2. **Install** per the maker: **flashing kit for the roof covering**, **insulation collar**, **underlay connection with a collar**, **airtight inner seal with tape**, **vapour-barrier connection**.
3. **Height ≈ 90–110 cm** to the sill for views and **an escape size** (check the rule).
4. **Shading:** an **external blind or an awning** (summer heat).
5. **Test** for leaks (water test) and the **condensation line**.

## Element: attic insulation layers and vapour barrier
1. **Check** the structure and fix leaks first. **Seal air paths** (penetrations, hatch, wall tops).
2. **Layer 1** between the rafters or joists, **friction-fit mineral wool or wood fibre**, **no gaps or compression**.
3. **Layer 2** across the structure (to cut the thermal bridge), **total typically ≈ 20–30 cm**.
4. **Vapour-control layer on the warm side**, **taped at all joints and edges**, **a service cavity ≈ 3–5 cm** so cables and spots don't puncture it.
5. **Ventilation gap** (cold roof) **≥ 4 cm** open at the eaves and the ridge. **Keep it open.**
6. **Photograph** before closing.
