# Quantities: how to calculate

Always: take dimensions from `my-home/rooms/<room>.md`, write the formula with units, round **up** to whole bags/packs, and sanity-check the result. All consumption values are **typical**: the product's data sheet wins.

## Areas
- Floor area = length × width (subtract fixed built-ins that won't be tiled).
- Wall area to tile = perimeter × tiled height − door and window openings. Add reveals (window and niche sides) separately.
- Waterproofing area = whole shower floor + shower walls to ≥ 200 cm + the rest of the bathroom floor + a 15–20 cm upturn on the walls (+ tape lengths).

## Tiles
- Order = area × (1 + waste). Use **one** waste factor, applied once (never 10% and then 15% on top). Waste: straight lay 10%; diagonal, herringbone or strong pattern 15%; large format (≥ 60 cm) in a small room 10–15%.
- Buy all tiles of one batch at once (same shade/calibre) and keep 1–2 spare tiles for future repairs.

## Tile adhesive (cement-based, per m², applied)
| Notch (trowel) | Typical use | Consumption |
|---|---|---|
| 6 mm | small wall tiles | 2.5–3 kg/m² |
| 8 mm | tiles up to ~30 × 30 | 3.5–4 kg/m² |
| 10 mm | floor tiles ~60 × 60 | 4.5–5.5 kg/m² |
| 12 mm | large format, uneven substrate | 5.5–7 kg/m² |
Back-buttering adds ≈ 0.5–1 kg/m². Add 10% waste (mixing leftovers, bucket). Bags are typically 25 kg.

**Check example:** 6.16 m² floor × 5.5 kg/m² (10 mm notch + back-buttering) = 33.9 kg, + 10% = 37 kg → 2 bags of 25 kg.

## Screed (bagged ready-mix, cement)
- Dry material ≈ 2.0–2.2 kg per m² per mm of thickness. Example: 6.16 m² × 55 mm × 2.1 kg ≈ 711 kg → about 29 bags of 25 kg, + 5–10% = 31 bags.
- Edge strip = perimeter + doorways. PE sheet = floor area + 20 cm up the walls + overlaps.

## Grout
Consumption (kg/m²) ≈ (L + W) / (L × W) × T × J × 1.6, with tile length L, width W, thickness T and joint width J all in **mm**. Add 10–15%.
Example: 600 × 600 mm, T = 9, J = 3 → (1200 / 360000) × 9 × 3 × 1.6 ≈ 0.14 kg/m². Small mosaics and thick tiles need much more, so always calculate and don't use a flat rule. Epoxy grout comes in small packs: calculate exactly, since leftovers cure and are wasted.

## Liquid waterproofing (2 coats, typical)
- Membrane: ≈ 1.2–1.6 kg/m² for the minimum dry film thickness (the data sheet gives the thickness and the consumption).
- Primer: ≈ 0.1–0.2 kg (or l)/m².
- Sealing tape: perimeter of the wet zones + wall corners + around the drain flange, + 10%.
- Collars/sleeves: one per pipe penetration.

## Silicone
- One 300 ml cartridge fills about 12 m of a 5 × 5 mm joint (300 ÷ 25 = 12). Joint length = perimeter at the floor/wall junction + inner wall corners + around fixtures. Add 1 cartridge reserve.

## Sanity check before you answer
- Does the quantity fit a normal pack size (bags 25 kg, cartridges 300 ml)?
- Are the units consistent (kg/m² × m² = kg)?
- Does the result differ by more than a factor of 2 from the examples above? Then find the error.
