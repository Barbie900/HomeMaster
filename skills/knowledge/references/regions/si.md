# Region: Slovenia (SI)

> Regulations change. Present these as orientation and tell the user to check the current version (pis.si, gov.si, the municipality, or the electricity distribution company) before relying on them.

## Permits
- Interior renovation that doesn't touch the load-bearing structure, the façade/appearance or the use of the building usually counts as **maintenance work (vzdrževalna dela)** under the Building Act (Gradbeni zakon, GZ-1) and doesn't need a building permit.
- Removing or opening walls, changing the use of a space, or touching the façade/windows in apartment blocks: check with an architect or the municipality first. Load-bearing walls need a **structural engineer (statik)**.

## Apartment blocks (večstanovanjske stavbe)
- Works on **common parts** (vertical stacks, risers, façade, load-bearing walls) need the co-owners' consent through the **building manager (upravnik)** under the Housing Act (SZ-1).
- Follow the **house rules (hišni red)** for noisy work hours. Notify neighbours before demolition.

## Electrical
- Low-voltage installations must comply with the regulation on low-voltage electrical installations in buildings and the technical guideline TSG-N-002.
- The installation must be **inspected and measured by a qualified electrician**, who issues a measurement report, before use. Insurers may refuse claims without one.
- Work on the meter, main fuses or the grid connection: only the **electricity distribution company** or its authorised contractors.
- What HomeMaster can help with as DIY: planning the layout and zones, chasing, laying conduits and boxes, preparation. Connection, the distribution board and certification: say clearly that a licensed electrician is needed.

## Gas
- **Never DIY.** Only a registered gas installer, with the gas distributor's approval.

## Asbestos
- Asbestos-cement (*salonit*) is common in older buildings (roofs, façade panels, pipes, window sills). Removal needs a **registered contractor** and disposal through an **authorised collector**. Never cut, drill, sand or break it.

## Practical
- DIY chains and builders' merchants sell tile, adhesive and waterproofing systems. Bigger stores and specialist rental companies rent tools (wet tile saws, wall chasers, concrete grinders, CM meters). Mention rental as an option for tools used once.
- Construction waste: municipal collection centres (zbirni centri) take limited household amounts. Larger amounts need a skip (kontejner) from a licensed waste company.
- Climate: in an unheated building, winter slows the curing of screed, adhesive and waterproofing. Most products need +5 °C (substrate and air) or more. Check before planning winter works.

## Other rooms and outdoor works (orientation only, check the current rules)
- **Windows and façade:** replacing windows or changing the façade in apartment blocks or in protected areas needs consent (co-owners, municipality, heritage authority). Check before ordering.
- **Terraces, outdoor stairs, pergolas, fences, sheds:** many are classified as simple or non-demanding structures with their own permit or notification rules, which depend on size, height, roofing and the distance to the plot boundary. Ask the municipality before building.
- **Stairs, railings, drop heights:** the building rules set minimum handrail and guard requirements. Check the current regulation.
- **Chimneys, wood stoves and fireplaces:** a **chimney sweep (dimnikar)** must inspect and approve a new or changed flue before use. Combustion air for airtight houses needs a plan.
- **Heat pumps, boilers and gas:** installation, commissioning and gas work only by registered installers.
- **Insulation and subsidies:** subsidies for insulation, windows and heat pumps (for example through the public environmental fund, Eko sklad) usually have documentation and sometimes certified-contractor conditions. **Check before starting works.**
- **Radon:** Slovenia has areas with elevated radon. The radiation protection authority (URSVS) publishes information and maps. Measure in basements and ground-floor rooms, especially if you convert them to living space.
- **Sauna, hot tub, pool:** the electrical installation by an authorised electrician. A pool or a larger wellness structure may need a permit. Check the municipality and the insurer.
- **Basement conversion:** a change of use to living space is subject to the building rules (height, daylight, ventilation, fire safety). Check before you plan.
- **Waste:** old windows, insulation and treated wood may need separate disposal at a collection centre.
- **Roof and attic:** changing the roof shape, adding dormers or roof windows, or converting an attic to living space is subject to the building rules (and possibly a permit). Work at height is regulated for professionals and dangerous for everyone: use scaffolds or certified fall protection. Asbestos-cement roofs (*salonit*) need a registered contractor.
- **Facade:** insulating or changing a facade often needs a permit or notification, consent of co-owners in apartment blocks and respect for heritage rules. Subsidies (e.g. through Eko sklad) usually require certified products or contractors. **Check the current conditions before the works.**
- **Garden structures:** sheds, carports, pergolas, fences and retaining walls are classed by size and use. Distances to the boundary and heights matter. Ask the municipality.
- **Pools:** a pool usually needs a permit above a certain size, and has safety barrier and electrical rules. Discharge of pool water is regulated. Ask the municipality and the electrician.
- **Chimney sweeping (dimnikarska služba):** in Slovenia chimney sweeping and inspections are a regulated service. Intervals and the approval of new or changed flues follow the regulation. Talk to a registered chimney sweep before you buy a stove. Gas and oil appliances only by registered installers.
- **Garden water and wells:** drawing groundwater from a well or borehole may need a water right or a notification. Rainwater tanks are usually unproblematic, but connecting garden water to the drinking supply needs a backflow preventer. Check the water utility.
- **Trees and hedges:** felling of protected or large trees and hedge distances to the boundary follow national and municipal rules. Ask the municipality before felling.
- **Smart home and data:** cameras on private property that film public space or neighbours are restricted by data-protection rules. The Information Commissioner (Informacijski pooblaščenec) publishes guidance. Keep cameras on your own plot.
- **Solar panels, batteries, EV chargers:** connected through the electricity distribution company with a notification or approval, installed and tested by authorised installers. Check the conditions before you buy.
- **Indoor stairs:** the building rules set minimum geometry and handrail requirements. For a new stair opening in the floor, an engineer and a permit.
