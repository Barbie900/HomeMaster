# Room type: garden (landscaping)

Type id: `garden`. The whole plot as a place to live in: soil, lawn, beds, trees, hedges, paths, lighting, water.

## What makes it different
- **Order of work matters:** machines, deliveries, hard surfaces first, soft landscaping last.
- **Plants take years:** the first two years of watering decide the result.
- **Soil, sun and water** decide what works. A plant in the wrong place never thrives.
- **Sleeves for irrigation, power, lighting and data** under every future path, drive and terrace.
- **Boundary and tree rules** (heights, distances, protected trees).

## Decide first
- Uses and zones (play, dining, utility, vegetables, privacy, wildlife)
- Hard surfaces (terrace, paths, drive) and structures, their levels and falls
- Water: irrigation or a watering plan, a rainwater tank
- Planting style and maintenance level
- Budget and the phases (one zone a year is fine)

## Task sequence (graph ids)
`survey-and-permits` → `garden-design-plan` → `site-drainage` → `garden-groundwork` → [`garden-structure-plan` → `garden-foundation` → `garden-structure-build`] → [`outdoor-substrate` → `paving` or `decking` or `outdoor-tiling`] → [`irrigation-plan` → `irrigation-install`] → `garden-planting`

## Room-specific rules and numbers
See `trades/landscaping.md`.
- Topsoil 15–20 cm for lawn, 25–40 cm for beds. Falls ≥ 2% away from buildings.
- Trees at about half to one times their mature height from buildings, more for thirsty species.
- Mulch 5–8 cm. Lawn seed ≈ 25–35 g/m². Autumn or spring planting.

## Extra interview questions
- Plot size, shape, slope, soil, sun, wind, existing trees and hedges, neighbours?
- What do you want to do in the garden? Who uses it (children, pets, elderly)?
- Where do the pipes and cables run? Is there a septic tank or a soakaway?
- Water source and budget for irrigation? Time for maintenance?

## Common mistakes
- Planting before the building works are finished.
- No sleeves under paving.
- Topsoil buried under rubble or subsoil.
- Trees too close to the house or the drains.
- No watering plan.
