# Room type: irrigation

Type id: `irrigation`. Watering the garden: hose and timer, drip, or a buried system with zones.

## What makes it different
- **Plan before you buy.** Flow and pressure decide how many sprinklers can run.
- **Backflow prevention** protects the drinking water.
- **Frost:** the system must be drainable and winterised.
- **Laid before surfaces:** sleeves under paving go in early.
- **Water rules and costs** (meters, restrictions, wells).

## Decide first
- Source: mains, well, rainwater tank
- What to water and how (drip for beds, trees and hedges, rotors or sprays for lawns)
- Zones and the controller (rain sensor, weather-based, soil sensors)
- Winterising method (drain valves, compressor blow-out)

## Task sequence (graph ids)
`garden-design-plan` → `irrigation-plan` → `garden-groundwork` (sleeves) → `irrigation-install` → `garden-planting`

## Room-specific rules and numbers
See `trades/irrigation.md`.
- Flow test with a bucket (l/min). A zone uses ≈ 70–80% of the available flow at most.
- Lawn ≈ 25 mm per week in summer. Drip needs ≈ 1–1.5 bar after a regulator and a 120–150 mesh filter.
- Pipes 25–40 cm deep, with drain points at the low spots.
- Backflow preventer, outdoor-rated and RCD-protected power, licensed electrician for mains.

## Extra interview questions
- Mains pressure and flow (bucket test)? Well or tank?
- Garden size, lawn vs. beds vs. vegetables? Sun and soil type?
- Existing taps, a garden meter, water bills?
- Frost in winter? Where can you drain and store the controller?
- Smart home or app control wanted?

## Common mistakes
- No flow measurement, no backflow preventer, no drain points, no filter on drip, no sleeves, no plan of the buried pipes, daily shallow watering.
