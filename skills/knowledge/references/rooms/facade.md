# Room type: facade

Type id: `facade`. The external walls: insulation, render, plinth, and the details at windows, roof and ground.

## What makes it different
- A **scaffold job at height**, weather-dependent, heavy materials.
- **Detailing** (corners, windows, plinth, eaves) decides durability, not the flat areas.
- **Permits, neighbours and heritage rules** apply to what the street sees.
- **Subsidies and warranties** often need certified work.
- It **ties together** the roof, the windows, the gutters, the ground drainage and the interior insulation.

## Decide first
- Insulate (ETICS or ventilated cladding) or only repair the render?
- Windows replaced in the same project, and their position in the wall
- Insulation material and thickness (EPS, mineral wool) and the fire requirements for the height
- DIY scope (e.g. a low building with a rented scaffold) vs. a contractor
- Colour and finish, and the plinth treatment

## Task sequence (graph ids)
`survey-and-permits` → `roof-assessment` / `roof-repair` → `site-drainage` → `windows-install` → `facade-preparation` → `facade-etics` → `facade-render` → `electrical-fit-out` (outdoor lights, meters, with a licensed electrician)

## Room-specific rules and numbers
See `trades/facade.md` for the system, materials, method and the quantities.
- ETICS insulation typically 10–20 cm, one tested system from one maker.
- Base coat ≈ 3–5 mm with mesh (overlap ≥ 10 cm, diagonal strips at the openings), cure before the finish render.
- **+5 to +25 °C, no rain or frost for ≥ 24–48 h**, no direct sun on fresh render.
- The plinth insulation below ground level (≈ 30 cm), with the render clear of the soil.

## Extra interview questions
- Wall construction (brick, concrete, stone, block, wood) and the age of the render?
- Cracks, hollow render, damp, algae, stains? Any asbestos panels?
- Number of storeys and height of the eaves? Space for a scaffold (distance to the fence, neighbours)?
- Windows, shutters, blinds, balconies, and the roof overhang details?
- Heritage area, apartment block or boundary issues?

## Common mistakes
- Insulating before the roof, windows and drainage are done.
- Mixed systems, cross joints, no diagonal mesh at the windows.
- Render touching the ground.
- Working in rain, frost or hot sun.
