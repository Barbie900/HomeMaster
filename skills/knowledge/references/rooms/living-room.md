# Room type: living room

Type id: `living-room`. Dry room. Large, many openings, the room where floor, light and acoustics matter most.

## What makes it different
- **Large areas**: floor, walls and ceilings dominate cost and time. Flatness, expansion joints and skim quality show.
- **Heating, glazing and acoustics** decide comfort. Big windows, high ceilings.
- Often has a **stove or fireplace**, TV/media wall, and an open link to the kitchen or the terrace.
- Floors above others: impact sound.

## Decide first
- Floor covering and whether it runs through to other rooms (door thresholds, level changes)
- Heating emitters (radiators or UFH), and the heat source (house-wide decision)
- Wall for the TV or media and heavy furniture (backing in drywall)
- Stove or fireplace, chimney, hearth protection, combustion air
- Lighting scenes and switching (dimmers, multiple switch points), network and TV outlets, blinds
- Doors and openings (glass doors, sliders) and the terrace connection

`survey-and-permits` → [`windows-install`] → [`insulation`] → [`demolition`] → [`partitions-and-ceilings`] → `electrical-rough-in` → `heating-distribution` → [`underfloor-heating`] → `wall-plaster-repair` → [`screed`] → `wall-finish` → `painting` → `floor-covering-dry` (or `floor-tiling`) → `door-install` → `skirting-and-trim` → `electrical-fit-out`

## Room-specific rules and numbers
- **Sockets and data:** plan generously (typical 8–12 sockets around the room, multiple per wall at TV and sofa positions, 2–3 data points, ceiling and floor boxes if needed). Cabling is cheap before plaster and expensive after.
- **Heating:** size the emitters for the planned insulation. UFH + parquet: check the total thermal resistance (flooring.md).
- **Stove / fireplace:** the flue, hearth and combustion air are regulated. Chimney-sweep inspection and sign-off before first fire; airtight houses need a dedicated air supply. Not DIY for the flue. See `rooms/chimney.md`.
- **Acoustics:** impact sound from the floor above or to the room below: decoupled screed and underlay, carpet or elastic layers, mineral wool in drywall.
- **Sun and glare:** exterior blinds or shading before the first summer. Overheating is easier to avoid than to fix.
- **Open to kitchen:** extractor plan, cooking smell, noise, a floor that works under both.

## Extra interview questions
- Floor now (screed, timber joists, tiles) and how much height there is to the door thresholds?
- Stove or fireplace wanted? Chimney present and its condition?
- TV on the wall, a projector, speakers (cable routes)?
- Direct terrace access? Doors to the garden (thresholds, watertightness)?
- UFH existing or planned? Heat pump?

## Common mistakes
- Laying the floor before painting and before doors, then damaging it.
- Underestimating dust from skimming and sanding.
- Too few sockets and no data cabling.
- Insufficient expansion gaps and joints in large floating floors.
- Forgetting ventilation after new airtight windows.
