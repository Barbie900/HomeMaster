# Room type: pool

Type id: `pool`. The general pool guide, and the one for an **indoor pool**. For an outdoor pool use `outdoor-pool` (it adds siting, heating, the cover and the winter routine). A construction project with drowning, electrical, structural, chemical and groundwater risks.

## What makes it different
- **Child safety:** barrier or cover is part of the pool, not an extra.
- **Groundwater:** an emptied pool can float or collapse.
- **Electrics** in a wet zone: licensed electrician and the pool rules.
- **Technical room, chemicals, winter** are ongoing duties.
- **Indoor pools** are building-physics projects (humidity, ventilation).

## Decide first
- Type (above-ground, liner, prefab shell, concrete, natural pond, indoor) and builder
- Size, depth, location, access for machines
- Permit and barrier rules
- Heating (heat pump, solar), cover, treatment (chlorine, salt, UV)
- Power, water, drain, wastewater, technical room

## Task sequence (graph ids)
`survey-and-permits` → `pool-plan` → `site-drainage` → `pool-structure` → `pool-technical` → `outdoor-substrate` → `outdoor-tiling` or `paving` or `decking` (surrounds) → `electrical-fit-out` (licensed, to the pool rules)

## Room-specific rules and numbers
See `trades/pool.md`.
- Filter and pump turn the volume over in ≈ 4–8 h. One skimmer per ≈ 25–40 m².
- pH 7.2–7.6, free chlorine ≈ 0.5–1.5 mg/l, alkalinity ≈ 80–120 mg/l (typical, follow the system).
- Fence ≈ 1.2–1.5 m with a self-closing, self-latching gate, or a safety cover.
- Surrounds fall ≥ 2% away from the pool, slip rating R11–R12 outdoors.
- Electrics: zones, RCD 30 mA, bonding, 12 V lights, licensed.

## Extra interview questions
- Plot, soil, groundwater, slope, trees, overhead lines, access for an excavator and crane?
- Children, pets, neighbours? What barrier is required here?
- Use: swimming, play, relaxing? Season of use? Heated?
- Power supply and a place for the technical room?
- Budget for running costs?

## Common mistakes
- No barrier, ignoring groundwater, DIY electrics, mixing chemicals, no pipe pressure test, backfilling without water.
