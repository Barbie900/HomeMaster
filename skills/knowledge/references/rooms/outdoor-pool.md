# Room type: outdoor pool

Type id: `outdoor-pool`. A pool in the garden: in-ground, above-ground, natural pond or swim spa. The indoor pool is covered by the `pool` type.

## What makes it different
- **Sun, wind and trees** decide how much heating and cleaning it needs.
- **Heat loss:** a cover and a heat pump (or solar) make the season longer.
- **Seasonal duties:** opening, closing and winterising are part of owning it.
- **Child safety, groundwater, electrics and chemicals** as for every pool.
- **Neighbours:** noise of pumps, lights at night, wastewater.

## Decide first
- Type and builder (above-ground, liner, prefab shell, concrete, natural pond, swim spa)
- Site (sun ≥ 6 h, wind protection, away from big trees, level and drained) and the permit
- Heating (heat pump, solar), cover, treatment (chlorine, salt, UV, natural)
- Surrounds (tiles, pavers, decking) and the barrier or cover
- Power, water, drainage, technical room and the winter routine

## Task sequence (graph ids)
`survey-and-permits` → `pool-plan` → `site-drainage` → `pool-structure` → `pool-technical` → `pool-heating-and-cover` → `outdoor-substrate` → `paving` or `decking` or `outdoor-tiling` (surrounds) → `electrical-fit-out` (licensed) → `pool-season-care`

## Room-specific rules and numbers
See `trades/pool.md`, section "Outdoor pool specifics".
- ≥ 6 hours of sun, the pool at least a tree's height away from big trees.
- A cover cuts heat loss by roughly 50–70%. Heat pump rough start ≈ 0.15–0.25 kW per m³ (then the maker's tables).
- Variable-speed pump saves about half the pumping electricity.
- Natural pond: regeneration zone ≈ 30–50% of the area, no chemicals, specialist design.
- Close and open the pool with a written routine. Never empty an in-ground pool in high groundwater or frost.

## Extra interview questions
- Plot size, orientation, trees, wind, slope, groundwater, neighbours' distance?
- Pool size and use (swimming, play, relaxing). Season length wanted? Heated?
- Children, pets? The fence or cover plan?
- Power supply and technical room? Water source and the drain for backwash?
- Budget for running costs?

## Common mistakes
- A shady, windy site, an oversized pool for the heating, no cover.
- Skipping the barrier, DIY electrics, ignoring groundwater, no winter plan.
