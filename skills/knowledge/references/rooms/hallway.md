# Room type: hallway / entrance area

Type id: `hallway`. Circulation and arrival. High wear, dirt and moisture (shoes, rain, snow), and the route for materials during the whole renovation.

## What makes it different
- **It is used for everything else's works**, so finish it **last** and protect it until then.
- **Wear and dirt:** the floor needs to be tough, easy to clean, and ideally with an integrated dirt-trapping mat zone.
- **Entrance door:** security, thermal bridging, threshold, drainage of rain, and a canopy.
- **Escape route and fire safety:** keep it clear. Smoke detector. Check requirements for the stair.
- Often holds the **main panel, meter and manifolds**: access must stay free (clearances in front of the panel).
- Often connects to stairs and other floors.

## Decide first
- Floor: tile or dense vinyl/hardwood, with a recessed mat well (typically ≥ 80 cm along the walking direction)
- Entrance door: replace? Threshold type (low or barrier-free), canopy
- Storage and coat/shoe zone; where heavy storage fixes to walls (backing)
- Lighting: motion or presence sensors, night light
- Panel access and any modules to add

## Task sequence (graph ids)
`survey-and-permits` → [`windows-install`] (entrance door) → `electrical-rough-in` → `wall-plaster-repair` → [`screed`] → `wall-finish` → `painting` → `floor-tiling` or `floor-covering-dry` → `door-install` → `skirting-and-trim` → `electrical-fit-out`

## Room-specific rules and numbers
- **Clear access** to the electrical panel (typically ≥ 80 cm free in front, and nothing hung in the way). A door or cover is fine if it opens fully.
- **Washable paint** (wet scrub class 1–2) up to 120–150 cm, or wall protection in high-contact areas.
- **Floor transitions** to each room at the same level where possible. A bevelled profile where it isn't.
- **Entrance threshold:** water must not run in. Slope outward, sealed, and a drainage channel if there is a step-free exterior.
- **Stairs inside:** riser and tread consistency, handrail, and non-slip nosings. A permit or an engineer for structural changes. See `rooms/indoor-stairs.md`.

## Extra interview questions
- Where does the delivery and debris route go? Is there another way?
- Is the entrance door staying? Any problems with draughts, water, security?
- Meter, panel and manifolds here? Anything to add or move?
- Stairs to other floors? Cellar entrance?

## Common mistakes
- Finishing the hallway before the other rooms.
- A floor that is slippery when wet.
- No dirt-trapping zone, so the whole home gets dirty.
- Panel blocked by a wardrobe.
