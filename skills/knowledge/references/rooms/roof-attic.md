# Room type: roof and attic

Type id: `roof-attic`. The envelope on top: rain-tightness, ventilation, insulation, and, if converted, the highest-risk living space in the house.

## What makes it different
- **Working at height.** The biggest DIY hazard in the whole project. Fall protection and a second person are not optional.
- **The roof comes first.** Interior work is wasted if the roof leaks.
- **Hidden damage:** condensation and leaks show up years later as rot.
- **Asbestos-cement roofs** (*salonit*) are common on older buildings: no walking, drilling, cutting or pressure washing.
- **Conversion to living space** means permits, engineer, escape route, insulation and summer overheating.

## Decide first
- Is it **repair**, **insulation of the attic floor** (unused attic) or **conversion to living space**?
- Who does what: roofer (re-roofing, structure, chimney, roof windows) vs. you (inspection, small repairs, interior work)
- Roof type (cold or warm roof), covering, pitch, spans
- Roof window sizes and positions, escape route, stair
- Insulation concept (vapour control, airtightness, ventilation gap)

## Room-specific rules and numbers
See `trades/roof.md` for the inspection checklist, safe working, repairs and the conversion details.
- Timber moisture ≥ 20% is a risk. Daylight in the attic roof is a leak.
- **Cold roof:** a ventilated gap ≥ 4 cm. **Warm roof:** a vapour-open underlay and an airtight inner layer.
- **Attic floor insulation** (unused attic) is the cheapest, biggest energy saver (≈ 20–30 cm).
- **Conversion:** headroom (typically ≥ 2.2–2.5 m over part of the floor), an engineer for loads and openings, a fixed stair, escape window, summer overheating plan.

## Extra interview questions
- Roof covering and age? Any leaks, stains, daylight, sagging?
- Is the attic floor insulated? Is the attic ventilated? Any roof windows now?
- Plan: leave unused, insulate the floor, or convert?
- Height under the ridge and at the knee walls? Stairs to the attic?
- Is there asbestos-cement (*salonit*) anywhere?

## Common mistakes
- Working alone or without edge protection.
- Fixing a ceiling stain without finding the leak.
- Insulating over damp or leaking timber.
- No airtight layer on the warm side.
- Blocking the ventilation gap.
